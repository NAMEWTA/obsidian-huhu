stdio使用有界单writer/排队字节。Linux/Darwin为继承OS pipe复制owned nonblocking pollable descriptor，deadline返回前实际Write已停止；Windows专用锁定线程执行同步WriteFile，以CancelSynchronousIo取消并等完成，controller ack后才能回线程池，Write/Close生命周期串行。OS拒绝停止时进程有限时fail-stop，不释放覆盖活写的permit或无限阻塞撤权；Windows此路径仅编译/代码核对，未声称实跑。实现依据微软官方[取消I/O](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/canceling-pending-i-o-operations)、[CancelSynchronousIo](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/ioapiset/nf-ioapiset-cancelsynchronousio)、[CloseHandle](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/handleapi/nf-handleapi-closehandle)、[WriteFile](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/fileapi/nf-fileapi-writefile)，没有复制第三方实现。
